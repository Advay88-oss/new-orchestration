# Positioning directive — infrastructure-led, dual B2B + B2C

**A standing directive. Every arc, every post, every launch plan honours it.**

## The directive

User acquisition continues, but it is no longer the headline. The **major
emphasis is the infrastructure Vanna is building**: composable credit rails that
are **scalable, flexible, reliable, and versatile**, and that serve **both B2B
and B2C** from one system — businesses embedding credit into their own product,
and end users (and their agents) getting a credit account — **without changing
the underlying system**.

Position Vanna as the *solution both ecosystems build on*, not as one more app.

## How this shapes the three arcs

The arcs still compete; this lens sits over all of them:

- [Capital efficiency](/arcs/capital-efficiency.md) → the *same rails* let one
  business unify margin and one consumer stretch collateral. One system, both.
- [Risk relief](/arcs/risk-relief.md) → the risk engine is infrastructure a
  business runs its book on *and* a consumer relies on. Same guardian, both sides.
- [Agentic credit](/arcs/agentic-credit.md) → "Morpho gives agents a market;
  Vanna gives them a balance sheet" is an *infrastructure* claim. Keep it there.

## The claim discipline this creates

Infrastructure language is the most tempting place to over-claim, and Vanna is on
[testnet](/company/constraint.md). So:

- **Say (design intent, present tense OK):** "one system for B2B and B2C",
  "composable credit infrastructure", "designed to scale", "the rails, not the app".
- **Never say (proven production, which testnet cannot back):** "production-grade",
  "enterprise-grade", "battle-tested", "proven at scale", "99.9% uptime",
  "SLA-backed". Rule `P13-infra-overclaim` blocks these.
- Scalability and reliability are stated as **how it is built**, never as **how
  it has performed in production** — because it has not performed in production.

## What "both B2B and B2C" means concretely for content

| Audience | The infrastructure story |
|---|---|
| Businesses | Embed undercollateralized credit in your app; Vanna runs the risk engine; your brand, front to back. API + SDK, no lending book to build. |
| Institutions | Route liquidity, trade on unified portfolio margin, segregated accounts. |
| Consumers | Your own credit account with hard limits; use it, borrow, build a track record. |
| Agents | A balance sheet with policy-bounded limits. |

One system underneath all four. That sameness *is* the pitch.
