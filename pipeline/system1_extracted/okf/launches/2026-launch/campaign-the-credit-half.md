# Vanna Launch Campaign — "The Credit Half"

**What we are launching.** Not mainnet (testnet only — no token, no audit, no
TVL). We are launching the **developer moment**: Vanna as the credit layer for AI
agents, with **testnet developer access + a design-partner program**. This is the
one thing that can genuinely go live now, and it acts on the audit's #1
correction — *lead with the developer wedge.*

**The gated finale.** The full protocol launch stays gated on audited mainnet.
This campaign builds the narrative, the developer pipeline, and the design
partners that convert the day mainnet ships.

---

## 1. Campaign objective

- **Primary:** recruit design partners / testnet integrators building autonomous
  agents that need credit (a real Phase-0 conversion).
- **Secondary:** own the sentence *"agents can pay, they can't borrow — Vanna is
  the credit half."* (category authority).
- **North star (Phase 0):** integrators in pipeline + share-of-voice on agentic
  credit — **not** followers.

## 2. Audience / ICP

- **Primary:** agent + DeFi developers building autonomous agents that need to
  borrow, hold a balance sheet, act under limits. On X/FinTwit, dev Discords,
  GitHub, HN.
- **Secondary:** protocols/institutions wanting composable credit rails without
  a lending book.

## 3. Core idea + positioning

**Core idea:** *Payments for agents shipped. Credit didn't. Vanna is the credit
half — a balance sheet with hard, policy-bounded limits.*

**Positioning:** infrastructure-led, dual B2B+B2C; *"Morpho gives agents access
to a lending market. Vanna gives them a balance sheet."*

**Hook (learned signal: statement/contrarian lead):** *"Payments were the easy
half."* / *"Give an agent a card and it can spend. Give it a balance sheet and it
can build."*

**Claim discipline (testnet):** designed-to-scale, one-system-for-B2B-and-B2C,
composable-credit-infrastructure — **never** production-grade/proven-at-scale
(P13), **never** token/airdrop/TVL/mainnet-live, **never** MCP-native (retired)
or category-ownership of agent credit scoring.

## 4. Offer / incentive (gate-safe — NO token)

| Incentive | Impact | Cost | Abuse | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Early testnet access** | high (dev) | ~0 | low | **ship** |
| **Design-partner status** (higher limits, direct support, co-marketing) | high (dev) | ~0 | low | **ship** |
| **Ecosystem grant / co-build (Stellar)** | med | grant | low | pursue |
| Token / airdrop / points | — | — | — | **never** — gate blocks (P3-token) |

Reward is **status + access**, never a financial promise — the same honest line
the Robinhood teardown drew (queue-position, not cash).

## 5. Funnel (multi-touch, not ad→signup)

```
Founder build-in-public ("payments were the easy half")   ← awareness
        ↓
The thesis thread ("agents can pay, can't borrow")         ← interest
        ↓
Testnet docs + "gold your code can talk to" equivalent      ← consideration
        ↓
Design-partner outreach / apply for testnet access          ← CONVERSION (real, now)
        ↓
[MAINNET GATE] → live credit routed → ecosystem referral
```

The design-partner / testnet-access step is the **real Phase-0 conversion.**

## 6. Channels (prioritised — not every platform)

1. **X / FinTwit** — founder voice + the thesis + agentic-credit tailwind.
2. **Developer channels** (dev Discords, HN, GitHub) — testnet is the live wedge.
3. **DeFi + Stellar ecosystem** — design partners, co-marketing, grants.
4. **Technical docs / thought leadership** — trust for an infra product.
Deferred: paid, consumer social.
