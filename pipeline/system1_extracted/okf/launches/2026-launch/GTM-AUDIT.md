# Vanna GTM & Campaign Audit

Reviewed as a senior GTM team, under the
[Campaign Intelligence Doctrine](/../CAMPAIGN-INTELLIGENCE-DOCTRINE.md):
executed-not-opined, causal, business-not-vanity, inferences labelled.

## The finding that governs every score

**Sharp thesis, no floor.** Vanna's agentic-credit wedge — *"agents can pay,
can't borrow; Vanna is the credit half / their balance sheet"* — is genuinely
differentiated and timely. But Vanna is on **testnet**: no mainnet, no token, no
audit, no TVL, so **there is nothing to convert to**, and the publish path to
reach anyone is broken. Plan quality is high; live GTM readiness is low — the gap
is **product maturity (mainnet) + distribution (publish)**, not strategy. Same
shape as Auri, different blocker.

## A. Executive summary

Vanna is composable credit infrastructure with a strong, ownable category
sentence in a hot market (agents + money). **Positioning, competitive framing,
and claim discipline are strong.** But it cannot launch a product (testnet), has
no brand/distribution, and no working channel. Correct move: **developer +
ecosystem-led Phase 0** (own the agentic-credit narrative, recruit design
partners, ship the testnet integration story), gating the protocol launch on
audited mainnet.

| Strategy | Using it? | Note |

Mix is appropriate and restrained. Execution depth ≈ 0 — designed, not live.

  weak       partial     designed        DEAD             DEAD        —

Biggest weakness: **no floor** (mainnet). Second: **no top** (publish + no brand).
Stage needing attention: **not creative — product (mainnet) + a publish channel.**

## D. Platform audit

- **X/FinTwit:** right primary — founder voice + agentic-credit narrative +
  developer intent. Awareness→trust, not conversion. Blocked on publishing.
- **Developer channels + GitHub:** *under-prioritised* — testnet integration is
  the only live wedge; should lead.
- **DeFi/Stellar communities:** design-partner + ecosystem distribution. Value-first.
- **LinkedIn:** secondary (institutional infra thought leadership).
- **Consumer social:** **not now** — wrong audience for DeFi infra.

## E. Campaign types

Now: category-creation + problem-awareness (nascent). Add: **educational**
(unified margin, risk engine — trust for infra), **founder build-in-public**.
Not now: paid, referral-with-token (gate blocks), consumer campaigns.

## F. Schedule audit

Two-track (dev/narrative now, mainnet-gated launch) is **correct**. Correction:
**sequence developer/ecosystem harder first** — a testnet integration or a design
partner is a real Phase-0 conversion; reach is not.

## G. Competitor intelligence

- **Morpho:** shipped MCP+CLI mainnet — retires "MCP-native"; position on the
  *balance sheet*, not MCP. They're the severe threat.
- **Agent-credit-scoring field** (Kojiru, ERC-8004, Visa TAP): **do not claim
  ownership;** Agent Score stays future tense. What NOT to copy: any
  category-ownership claim — the gate blocks it (`R-category-claim`).

## H. Real-world campaign research (honesty-labelled)

DeFi protocol GTM that worked (inferred economics — on-chain TVL is public,
conversion attribution is not): **developer-first + ecosystem co-marketing +
narrative ownership** (e.g. how lending protocols grew via integrations, not
consumer ads). Adapt: recruit builders and own the sentence; **do not** buy
reach. Every un-sourced conversion figure = inferred, not asserted.

## I-J. Recommended strategy + architecture

Own "the credit half of agent payments," recruit design partners, ship testnet
integrations, gate mainnet on audit.
```
Agentic-credit tailwind → founder narrative ("payments were the easy half")
        ↓                                    ↘
Developer/testnet integration (live wedge)     Design-partner / ecosystem pipeline
        ↓                                            ↓
Testnet integrations = real Phase-0 conversion   [MAINNET GATE] → live credit → referral
```

## K. 30/60/90

  thread + docs; push the 3 gated assets.
- **30 days:** developer + design-partner outreach; agentic-credit content cadence;
  DeFi/Stellar community presence; instrument run_id.

  competitor teardowns (Morpho, Gearbox) into the KB.
- **90 days:** mainnet-readiness review (gated on audit); if audited, run the
  launch sequence; else keep compounding narrative + integrations.

## 10-point verdict

1. **Right:** the credit-wedge/balance-sheet positioning, competitor framing,
   claim discipline (no token/MCP/category overclaim), restrained mix, gating on
   mainnet.
2. **Missing:** working publish channel; developer/ecosystem as the *lead*;
   run_id; any live brand.
3. **Unnecessary now:** paid, consumer social, token incentives.
4. **Poorly structured:** developer/testnet wedge (the only live one) treated as
   secondary — should lead Phase 0.
5. **Prioritise:** unblock publishing → developer/ecosystem push → design partners.
6. **Remove:** dated mainnet language; any token/airdrop hint; MCP-native.
7. **Test:** hook (credit vs balance-sheet vs infra) + segment lead.
8. **Immediately:** publish path + thesis thread + push gated assets.
9. **Next week:** developer/design-partner outreach + run_id.
10. **Next month:** first real learn cycle + Morpho/Gearbox teardowns.

**Bottom line:** senior-grade plan, near-zero live execution; blockers are mainnet
(product) and publishing (distribution), not strategy. Same medicine as Auri: fix
those two and this is a strong GTM.
