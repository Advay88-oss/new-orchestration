# Vanna GTM Launch Blueprint — 2026

**The one strategic decision this plan turns on.** Vanna is on Stellar Soroban
**testnet** — no mainnet, no token, no audit, no TVL. A user cannot deploy real
capital. So, exactly like Auri's missing fiat rails, **Vanna's funnel has no
floor**: there is no live product to convert to yet. Any plan that assumes a
public launch is fiction.

This blueprint is built around what is real now:
- **Now (Phase 0):** **developer + ecosystem-led** growth — win the *category
  conversation* on agentic credit, ship the developer/testnet integration story,
  and build authority + a design-partner pipeline. No token, no mainnet needed.
- **At mainnet (Phase 1):** the full protocol launch, when contracts are audited
  and live.

Lead the narrative and recruit builders now; launch the protocol at mainnet.

| Horizon | Objective | Target (illustrative) |

Phase 0 is measured in **pipeline and authority**, not TVL (impossible on
testnet) — the same honesty as Auri's pre-rails phase.

## 2. Market & audience

Agentic payments are shipping (agents getting cards, wallets, payment rails), but
**agent credit is not** — an agent can pay, it cannot borrow, hold a balance
sheet, or answer for a loan. That gap is the market. Tailwind: the whole "agents
+ money" narrative is hot on FinTwit; regulators and incumbents are legitimising
autonomous-agent finance.

**ICP:**
- **Primary — agent/DeFi developers** building autonomous agents that need credit,
  not just payments. On X, dev Discords, DeFi/agent communities.
- **Secondary — protocols & institutions** wanting composable credit rails
  (unified margin, risk engine) without building a lending book.
- **Tertiary — DeFi-native users/agents** (the B2C side of the same rails).

- **Blend** — Stellar-native lending; ecosystem-adjacent.

## 4-5. ICP → problem → 6-7. Positioning

**Core positioning** ([/positioning/infrastructure-thesis.md](/positioning/infrastructure-thesis.md)):
*infrastructure-led, dual B2B + B2C* — composable credit rails that serve
businesses embedding credit and end users/agents getting a credit account, from
one system.

**Lead line:** *"Payments were the easy half. Vanna is the credit half."* /
*"Morpho gives agents a lending market. Vanna gives them a balance sheet."*

**Messaging discipline (testnet):** say "designed to scale", "one system for B2B
and B2C", "composable credit infrastructure"; **never** "production-grade",
"proven at scale", "99.9% uptime" (rule `P13-infra-overclaim`), and **never**
token/airdrop/TVL/mainnet-live (hard prohibitions). White space: *"an agent's
balance sheet, with hard policy-bounded limits"* — no incumbent says this.

## 22-33. Funnel, budget, KPIs, experiments, measurement, loop

- **Funnel:** Awareness (X/dev) → Interest (docs/testnet) → **[mainnet gate]** →
  Integration → live credit routed → ecosystem referral. The **integration step
  is the current dead-end** until mainnet.
- **Budget:** Phase 0 cheap (content in-house); spend on **ecosystem/partnerships**
  (the distribution Vanna needs), paid only post-mainnet.
- **KPIs (Phase 0, not vanity):** design-partners/integrators in pipeline,
  developer docs engagement, category share-of-voice on agentic credit. North
  star flips to **integrations routing real credit** at mainnet.
- **Experiments:** hook (credit-wedge vs balance-sheet vs infrastructure);
  segment lead (dev vs protocol). *Learned so far: statement/contrarian hooks
  lead.*
- **Loop:** measure → `learn.py` → INSIGHTS → generation (wired, cross-company
  with Auri at the feature level, V6-safe).
