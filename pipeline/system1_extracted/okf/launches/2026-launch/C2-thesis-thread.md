# C2 — X thread: the thesis (interest)
**Audience:** agent/DeFi devs · **Funnel:** interest · **Objective:** category authority · **CTA:** read the docs / follow

1/ Payments for AI agents shipped this year. Agents have cards, wallets, rails. Credit didn't. An agent can pay — it still can't borrow, hold a balance sheet, or answer for a loan. That gap is the whole game.

2/ A card is spend-only. It draws down what the agent already has. It can't take a position it settles later, can't be trusted with a limit, can't build. Payments were the easy half.

3/ The hard half is credit: a balance sheet with hard, policy-bounded limits. Borrow against collateral, act autonomously, but never exceed what the policy allows. That's an account, not a card.

4/ Morpho gives agents access to a lending market. Vanna gives them a balance sheet — composable credit rails, for the businesses deploying agents and the agents themselves. One system, both sides. On Stellar testnet.

5/ Building agents that need to borrow, not just spend? This is the credit half. Testnet access is opening to design partners — read the thesis, come build.
