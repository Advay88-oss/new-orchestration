# What DeFi categories actually publish — and what that obliges Vanna to do

Extracted 2026-09-24 from the Notion page **"DEFI COMPETITORS
(PATTERN/CATEGORY)"** (Projects, last edited 2026-09-09), a study of twelve
protocols across six categories. This is the content strategist's raw
material: it says which post types are *mandatory* in a category and which are
a choice.

> **The one idea:** every category has one big fear its customers have before
> they hand over money. The content that answers that fear is mandatory — you
> publish it or you are not taken seriously. Everything else is your choice.
> **Copy the mandatory. Invent the optional.**

---

## The six fears

| Category | The customer's fear | So both players must publish |
|---|---|---|
| Lending | "Will your rules blow up?" | Their risk rules, and the reasoning |
| Derivatives | "Can I get out when I want?" | Volume and depth numbers |
| DEX | "Does this thing actually work?" | Technical mechanism explanations |
| Staking | "Why you and not the other twenty?" | Anything that isn't the product |
| BTCFi | "Will I lose my Bitcoin?" | Custody proof, over and over |
| Yield / Synthetic | "Where is the money coming from?" | Who holds your money |

Protocols studied: Aave, Morpho · Derive, Hyperliquid · Uniswap, Curve ·
Lido, ether.fi · Babylon, Lombard · Pendle, Ethena.

---

## Patterns that survive all six categories

| Pattern | Who runs it | Why it is universal |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone / metric post | 12/12 | A number is the only claim nobody argues with |
| Product or launch drop | 12/12 | Shipping is the one thing you always have to talk about |
| Partnership announcement | 12/12 | Borrowing the partner's audience is the cheapest distribution |
| Founder / named human voice | 11/12 | A company account cannot be trusted the way a person can. Lido is the only abstainer, deliberately |
| Recurring cadence | 10/12 | Predictability replaces volume. A fixed slot brings people back without chasing them |
| Token or economics content | 10/12 | Where fees exist, tying them to the token is a free recurring proof event |

**The two rarest patterns in the whole study**, both of which buy
disproportionate trust: third-party attestation (only Ethena) and publishing
your own misses (only Lido and the Uniswap Foundation). Both require
volunteering something that could embarrass you.

## Category-locked patterns

| Pattern | Mandatory in | Optional in | Forbidden in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy governance content | Lending, DEX | Derivatives, Staking | — |
| Points & incentive campaigns | BTCFi, Yield/Synthetic | Derivatives, DEX, Staking | **Lending** |
| Structured education | BTCFi, Yield/Synthetic | DEX | *(only an incumbent skips it)* |
| Custody & proof-of-reserve | BTCFi, Synthetic | Staking | — |

## Devices worth stealing

- **Numbered series.** "Builder Update #42", "Pendle Print #118", "Week 32,
  2026". Call it #42 and you have promised #43; miss it and everyone sees. It
  is a commitment device pointed at yourself.
- **Curve's candour.** It splits advertised yield into real trading fees vs
  token emissions that will stop — it literally tells you which half to
  distrust.
- **Morpho's floor framing.** The milestone is a floor, never a ceiling:
  "$5B is the new day one."
- **Hyperliquid's understatement.** An order-book screenshot captioned
  "quietly hit…". Zero adjectives in the weekly changelog.
- **Pendle's built-in calendar.** Every pool has an expiry, so the product
  generates its own content calendar. Everyone else has to invent a reason to
  post.
- **Babylon's contrast refrain.** "no wrapping, no bridging, no custody-loss",
  repeated until it becomes the brand.

## What this obliges Vanna to do

Nobody in the study does undercollateralised composable credit, so Vanna's
mandatory content cannot be copied — it has to be worked out. **Vanna's fear:**

> "Undercollateralised means you can be left holding bad debt. Why doesn't
> this get wiped out?"

**MANDATORY**

- **Mechanism transparency.** In lending, publishing your risk process is how
  you prove one exists. Vanna has ~38 verified mechanism claims — the rate
  curve, the health-factor derivation, liquidation at 1.1, the 10% surplus
  rationale. Publish the parameters **and why each one is set where it is**.
  *(The source notes this was previously mis-framed as "an attractive
  whitespace." It is the category's mandatory content.)*
- **Counterparty disclosure, on the Ethena template.** Vanna's credit spans
  venues; users will ask where their exposure actually sits. Numbered
  editions, fixed structure, exact figures, and a stated limitation about what
  the report does not yet cover.
- **Education.** Every category with an unfamiliar primitive runs literacy
  content. Vanna's primitive is unfamiliar to almost everyone.

**UNAVAILABLE**

- **Points campaigns.** Both lending players abstain for a structural reason:
  paid deposits in a credit market are *lower quality*, not just more
  expensive. A protocol extending undercollateralised credit has even more
  reason to avoid mercenary liquidity.
- **Milestone drops.** All twelve run them. Vanna cannot, honestly, until
  mainnet. That is a scheduling constraint, not a strategy problem — run the
  patterns that need no scale instead.
- **Hyperliquid's silence is not a model.** It is what winning sounds like
  afterwards.
