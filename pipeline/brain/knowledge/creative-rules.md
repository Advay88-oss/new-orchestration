# Vanna creative rules — posters and videos

The founder's rules, gathered from every correction on 2026-09-23..25. The
Motion Director, the image agent and both judges load this file on every run.
Rules the Coach (A13) learns from runs are appended to `learned-rules.md`, not
here — this file changes only when the founder changes a rule.

## The bar

- Every poster is at the level of the founder-approved exemplars in
  `pipeline/brain/visual_exemplars/` — the image model is shown them first.
- Posters are made by the image model directly from the design references and
  the approved posters. Nothing about the layout is drawn in code.
- Same quality and brand every run, never the same layout twice in a row: the
  Motion Director picks a different layout family and motion style each run.

## Brand

- The official Vanna lockup — the ribbon mark and the lowercase wordmark
  "vanna" — centred at the top. Never the old cube logo, never an invented mark.
- Dark ground with a soft violet glow low-left and a magenta glow high-right.
  Keep to Vanna's violet and magenta; avoid cyan and teal.
- A heavy, bold sans-serif headline, centred, in sentence case, under 9 words,
  with ONE key word or short phrase in a pink-to-violet gradient italic.
- One short grey subtitle line, under 14 words.
- A footer: a few bold white words, then a short grey caveat that says testnet.

## Composition

- Headline and subtitle on top, then 2-4 large, clearly separated glass cards,
  each holding one simple diagram, then the footer. Generous space. Nothing
  overlapping, nothing clipped, everything aligned.
- FLAT and face-on: 2D glass cards, flat icons, straight arrows. No isometric
  or 3D objects (chips, cubes, platforms seen at an angle).
- Few words. At most 6 diagram labels, each 1-4 words. Every word is drawn and
  every word is a chance to misspell.
- No markdown characters rendered as text (** __ `). Emphasis is the gradient word.

## Truth

- Vanna is on Stellar Soroban TESTNET. Never imply mainnet.
- Only true figures: 1.10x liquidation floor, 0.00014 XLM fixed gas, ~320ms
  indexing. No user counts, TVL, APY, percentages or prices.
- Other protocols (Blend, Aquarius, Soroswap) appear as plain text names only —
  never a logo or an icon for them. Never name a competitor; say "pooled lending".
- No coins, token logos or currency symbols anywhere.
- Cover every subject the founder's query names.

## Video (Veo 3.1)

- The video is the poster building itself: first frame the empty Vanna ground,
  last frame the finished poster, 10 seconds (8s of Veo, the final frame held).
- Camera completely locked off. Everything moves in the flat plane.
- Text appears in its final position, fully formed, correctly spelled, and
  never changes after. Nothing appears that is not in the last frame.
- Motion explains the idea: the problem side strains or fails, Vanna's side
  settles calmly.
- Every delivered video is downloadable.
