# Vanna early-access quest on Galxe (October 2026)

Source: the team's quest brief, 11 Oct 2026. Runs with the real margin waitlist launch.

**Title:** "Solana Traders, Ready to Unlock More Capital? Vanna early access"

**Description:** Vanna is bringing real margin to Solana. Borrow against your collateral and explore new
ways to put capital to work across DeFi. Join the early-access waitlist. 10 winners receive $100 USDC
each. What would you do first with real margin? Join early. Get ready to trade differently.

**Tasks**
1. Join the Vanna waitlist with your email; use the same email as your Galxe ID so the entry can be
   verified (Visit Link, then email verification).
2. Follow Vanna on X.
3. Like and repost the Real Margin thread on X.
4. Join the Vanna Telegram.
5. Trader quiz: which chain you mainly use; whether you trade perps, spot or use DeFi lending; what you
   would use real margin for.
6. Bonus: quote-post the Real Margin thread with "What would you do first if you had access to real
   margin on Solana?", tagging Vanna. The team reviews submissions by hand; selected answers may be
   featured on Vanna's channels.

**Setup:** required = waitlist signup, X follow, trader quiz, Telegram join; bonus = quote-post and a
verified referral. Reward: 10 winners × $100 USDC, subject to the published eligibility and
winner-selection rules. The verified waitlist signup is the main conversion.

**Rules for content**
- Say a task is verified only where the Galxe setup really verifies it (email matching and referrals
  are tested before launch).
- Always pair the reward with "subject to the published rules"; never promise more, never call it an
  airdrop or a token.
- The Solana build is a test app: the quest is open, the product is not live.
- The X handle, Telegram link, thread URL and Galxe URL are placeholders until the team confirms them.
