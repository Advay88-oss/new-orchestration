# Positioning directive — infrastructure-led, dual B2B + B2C

**A standing directive. Every arc, every post, every launch plan honours it.**

## The directive

User acquisition continues, but the **major emphasis is the infrastructure**:
Auri is not just a consumer gold app, it is **gold rails** — allocated, attested,
non-custodial gold that is **scalable, flexible, reliable, and versatile**, usable
by **both B2B and B2C** from one system. A consumer buys and uses gold; a business
builds on the same rails via the developer API — **without changing the underlying
system**.

Position Auri as the *rails other products build gold on*, not only an app.

## How this shapes the three arcs

- [Gold you can use](/arcs/use-your-gold.md) → the same rails that let a person
  spend gold let a business offer gold-backed features. One primitive, both.
- [Non-custodial](/arcs/self-custody.md) → "Auri holds $0" is an *infrastructure*
  property — it is true whether the user is a person or a platform.
- [Store of value](/arcs/store-of-value.md) → allocated, attested reserves are
  the trust layer both consumers and businesses stand on.

## The claim discipline this creates

- **The consumer product is live and evidenced** — allocated XAUT, BDO quarterly
  attestation, 22+ tonnes, non-custodial. These are [Tier A](/facts/tier-a.md),
  quotable.
- **The developer API / rails for B2B are advertised but were not verified**
  (behind login) — they are [Tier C](/facts/tier-c.md), **future tense only**
  until confirmed. Do not claim the B2B infrastructure is production-ready.
- **Never** say "production-grade", "enterprise-grade", "battle-tested",
  "proven at scale", "99.9% uptime", "SLA-backed" — rule `AU5-infra-overclaim`
  blocks these until there is evidence. Say "designed for B2B and B2C", "gold
  rails you can build on".
- Borrowing/leverage in any infrastructure framing still carries its liquidation
  risk (see [/company/constraint.md](/company/constraint.md)).

## What "both B2B and B2C" means concretely for content

| Audience | The infrastructure story |
|---|---|
| Consumers | Buy real gold, spend/send/borrow, non-custodial. Live today. |
| Businesses / developers | Build gold-backed features on Auri's rails via API. **Future tense until verified.** |
| Both | Same allocated, attested, non-custodial reserve underneath. |

The sameness of the reserve and the rails *is* the pitch — but keep the B2B side
in future tense until the API is confirmed.
