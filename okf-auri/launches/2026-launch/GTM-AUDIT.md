# Auri GTM & Campaign Audit

Reviewed as a senior GTM team (Growth Strategist + Performance Marketer + Brand
Strategist + CRO + Analyst). Applies the [Campaign Intelligence Doctrine](/../CAMPAIGN-INTELLIGENCE-DOCTRINE.md):
executed-not-opined, causal, business-not-vanity, and **every un-sourced number
labelled inferred.**

---

## The one finding that governs every score

**The strategy is strong. The funnel has no bottom.** Auri's fiat on-ramp
(ACH/Interac) and real KYC are not built — a user literally cannot fund an
account. And the current publishing path to reach anyone is broken (X blocks
browser automation). So today Auri can produce excellent *plans and assets* but
cannot **acquire at scale or convert at all.** Every score below is split
accordingly: **plan quality is high; live GTM readiness is low**, and that gap is
the whole story.

This is not a content problem. It is a **product-readiness + distribution**
problem, and no amount of campaign cleverness fixes a funnel with no floor. Say
it plainly to anyone who asks "why aren't we launching."

---

## A. Executive summary

Auri is a gold neobank with a genuinely differentiated wedge — *"cash against your
gold, no credit check, 3.75%, non-custodial"* — in a category being legitimised
top-down (FCA tokenized-gold rules, central banks buying). The **positioning,
competitive intelligence, and claim discipline are strong.** But the consumer
product cannot be funded yet, and there is no working publish channel, no brand,
and no distribution. The correct strategy is therefore **not a launch** — it is a
**two-track pre-launch**: ship the *developer API* (live, global, the only fully
serviceable segment today) and *seed a consumer waitlist* riding the category
tailwind, gating the real consumer launch on rails.

## B. Current strategy audit — what's actually being used

| Strategy | Using it? | How / gap |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-led / content-led growth | **Yes (nascent)** | gate-passed X posts + cards produced; not yet published live |
| Competitor-switching / comparison | **Yes** | "everyone sells gold, nobody lets you use it" — strong wedge |
| Waitlist strategy | **Designed, not live** | landing hero written (CA+US); no page deployed |
| Developer/PLG (API) | **Under-used** | the one live, global segment — should be the *lead*, currently secondary |
| Community-led | **Planned** | Reddit/diaspora — right for the ICP, not started |
| Referral | **Designed** | queue-position mechanic (from Robinhood teardown); Phase 1 for gold rewards |
| Paid / performance | **Correctly OFF** | no funded conversion + no converting funnel = premature; good call |
| Influencer / partnerships | **Planned, Phase 1** | the distribution Auri most lacks; deferred correctly |
| SEO / email / lifecycle | **Minimal** | email nurture designed; SEO absent |

**Verdict:** the *mix* is appropriate and admirably restrained (not "every channel
because it exists"). The failure is **execution depth = ~0** — almost everything
is designed, almost nothing is live.

## C. Funnel audit — where users enter, drop, convert

```
Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Click → Landing → Signup → Activation → Conversion → Retention → Referral
  weak       designed    partial        —        none      none      DEAD        DEAD        —          designed
```

- **Biggest weakness: the floor is missing.** Signup → **Fund** → Activation is a
  hard stop — no fiat rails. Everything upstream feeds a dead end.
- **Second weakness: the top.** No working publish path, no brand, one small
  personal account (4 followers) → awareness ≈ 0 at scale.
- **Stage needing most attention:** not "conversion optimisation" — it is
  **product readiness (rails)** and **a publish channel.** Optimising creative
  now is polishing a car with no engine.

## D. Platform audit (only where relevant — not every platform)

- **X:** right primary channel for the dev wedge + founder authority + the
  tokenized-gold narrative. Contribution should be Awareness→Trust→Intent, *not*
  conversion (no funnel floor). **Blocked on publishing.**
- **Developer channels (HN, Product Hunt, dev Discords):** *under-prioritised.*
  This is the only segment Auri can fully serve today — it deserves to lead.
- **Reddit:** high value for the credit-invisible ICP and objection-mining;
  value-first only, **no astroturfing.** Needs the browser bridge for depth.
- **LinkedIn:** useful for B2B/dev thought leadership (tokenized-gold-as-
  infrastructure). Secondary.
- **YouTube:** right format for a trust-hard product (self-custody explainer),
  but production cost is high — Phase 1.
- **Instagram / TikTok:** **not now** — no evidence of ICP fit; would be content
  for content's sake.
- **Paid search / Meta:** **not now** — no funded conversion to buy.

## E. Campaign-type audit

Currently: **comparison** + **problem-awareness** (nascent), **waitlist** and
**developer-launch** (designed). Should add: **educational** (the credit wedge,
self-custody — a trust-hard product needs it), **founder-led build-in-public**
(cheap, authentic, fits the tailwind). Should **not** run yet: paid-acquisition,
freemium, giveaway, retargeting (no floor to retarget toward).

## F. Schedule audit — is the sequencing right?

The blueprint's two-track schedule is **strategically correct**: dev launch now,
consumer T-0 **gated on rails, not dated.** The one correction: **the dev track
should be sequenced first and harder** — it is the only track that can complete a
funnel today (API signup is a real conversion; a consumer waitlist signup is
not). Reweight Phase 0 toward developer acquisition.

## G. Competitor intelligence (differentiators)

From the 22 dossiers + [threat radar](/../competitors/threat-radar-2026-08-11.md):
- **Wealthsimple (CA):** distribution-first launch inside an existing trusted app.
  Auri cannot copy this (no distribution). Learn: lead with the leg they lack
  (credit/self-custody).
- **Coinbase (US):** lends on Morpho from ~4% — architecturally one config change
  from Auri's product. **Auri's window is open now; it will not stay open.**
- **Ledn × Tether:** XAUT loans excluding CA/EU, **not the US** — a real,
  time-boxed threat. Speed matters.
- **What NOT to copy:** custodial trust-by-brand (Auri's whole pitch is the
  opposite), or crypto-native tone (Auri's audience explicitly is not).

## H. Real-world campaign research (executed, with honesty labels)

- **Robinhood waitlist** (teardown stored): referral-for-queue-position → ~$0 CAC,
  1M+ waitlist. **Measured:** waitlist size, ~$0 CAC (reported). **Inferred:**
  waitlist→funded conversion (not public). **Adapt:** copy the *mechanic* (queue
  position, not gold rewards — fraud/compliance) for Auri's pre-launch.
- **Revolut / PayPal:** cash-for-referral drove word-of-mouth growth. **Inferred**
  exact economics. **Do not copy** cash rewards for Auri pre-KYC (abuse).
- Honesty note: no Auri-space campaign's *conversion* numbers are public — every
  such figure here is labelled inferred, not asserted.

## I. Recommended GTM strategy

**Lead developer + seed consumer, gate the launch.** Win the category sentence
("gold you can use / borrow against, non-custodially"), the credit wedge, and
diaspora distribution — before Coinbase/Ledn move. Concretely:
1. **Now:** developer API as the hero (live, global, converts today) + founder
   build-in-public on the tokenized-gold tailwind + a real consumer waitlist.
2. **At rails:** the consumer launch, US+Canada, with the credit-wedge hook.

## J. Campaign architecture (adapted to Auri)

```
Tokenized-gold tailwind (FCA, central banks)      ← awareness, real not manufactured
        ↓
Founder build-in-public + the credit wedge         ← trust + intent
        ↓                         ↘
Developer API (live) ─► API signup    Consumer waitlist (queue-position referral)
  = REAL conversion today               = demand, gated on rails
        ↓                                        ↓
Integrator activation                    [RAILS GATE] → fund → activate → convert → refer
```

Two funnels; only the developer one has a floor today. Build accordingly.

## K. 30 / 60 / 90-day execution plan

**Next 7 days (unblock, or nothing else matters):**
1. Decide the publish path — **X API** (settle the credential path) or accept
   manual posting. Without this, awareness stays ≈ 0.
2. Stand up the **consumer waitlist page** (hero copy is written) + the **developer
   docs/landing** ("gold your code can talk to").
3. Publish the 5 gate-passed assets (manually if needed) + the dev thread.

**30 days:** developer acquisition push (HN/PH prep, dev-thread series, session-
key-security angle); founder build-in-public cadence on X; Reddit value-first
presence; waitlist referral (queue-position) live; instrument `run_id`.

**60 days:** measure what published (needs a working channel); first learn→improve
cycle on real own-post data; competitor launch teardowns (Wealthsimple, Coinbase)
into the KB; first launch-video spine (Remotion, no AI) if publishing is solved.

**90 days:** consumer-launch readiness review **gated on rails**; if rails land,
run the pre-launch → launch sequence; if not, keep compounding dev + waitlist +
authority. Do **not** date a consumer launch rails can't support.

`Hypothesis → A → B → KPI → Decision`, one variable:

## N. KPI & measurement framework

**Primary for Auri's stage** (not vanity): **API integrator signups** (the only
live conversion), **qualified waitlist signups**, **waitlist referral rate /
viral coefficient**, and **authority** (category share of voice / earned
mentions). **Not** followers/impressions as goals. Post-rails, north star flips
to **fund→first-action activation rate.** Blocked on `run_id` + a publish channel
+ analytics connectors.

**The number that matters: ~3/10 live readiness**, and it is gated by two things
neither of which is marketing — **rails (product) and a publish path
(distribution).**

## The 10-point verdict

1. **Right:** positioning/credit-wedge, competitor intel, claim discipline, the
   restrained channel mix, gating the launch on rails (honest).
2. **Missing:** a working publish channel; the developer track as the *lead*; a
   deployed waitlist page; `run_id`/attribution; any live brand.
3. **Unnecessary (now):** paid, Instagram/TikTok, retargeting, freemium — no floor
   to justify them.
4. **Poorly structured:** developer segment (the only live one) is treated as
   secondary — it should lead Phase 0.
5. **Prioritise:** unblock publishing → ship dev API GTM → waitlist page.
6. **Remove:** any dated consumer-launch language; any manufactured urgency.
7. **Test:** the hook and the segment-lead first.
8. **Execute immediately:** publish path decision + waitlist/dev pages + push the
   5 ready assets.
9. **Next week:** dev-acquisition cadence + `run_id`.
10. **Next month:** first real own-post learn cycle + competitor launch teardowns
    + Remotion video spine.

**Bottom line:** you have a senior-grade *plan* and near-zero *live execution
capability*, and the two blockers are product (rails) and distribution (publish),
not strategy. Fix those two and this becomes a genuinely strong GTM. Keep writing
plans without fixing them and the score never moves.
