# Gnosis Pay

Fully self-custodial Visa spending stablecoins from a Safe smart account — the architectural pattern Auri uses, on crypto.

**What they are.** IBAN, up to 5% cashback, across EEA/UK/CH/Argentina/Brazil — NOT US or Canada. Crypto collateral, no gold.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Proven self-custody card pattern, cashback

**Where Auri beats them.**
- No gold; not in Auri's launch markets; crypto-native brand

**Positioning.** Architectural rival, not asset rival — they proved the pattern on crypto.

**Watch for.** If Gnosis Pay adds XAUT collateral, they get Auri's product overnight.
