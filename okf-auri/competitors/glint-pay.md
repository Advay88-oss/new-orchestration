# Glint Pay

The clearest precedent for spending gold — a Mastercard debit card spending gold in 210 countries.

**What they are.** 0.5% buy/sell/FX; storage+insurance ~0.24%/yr. Custodial. No borrowing at all. No self-custody, no on-chain proof, no API.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Live card, years of history, spend-gold proven

**Where Auri beats them.**
- No credit line against gold
- Custodial
- No self-custody/proof/API

**Positioning.** Glint proves spend-gold is real. It never added credit — Auri's bet is that gap is still open.

**Watch for.** If Glint adds borrowing.
