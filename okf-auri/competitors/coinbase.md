# Coinbase

Lends USDC against BTC from ~4% APR, built on Morpho, running on Base. The architectural distance to Auri is close to zero.

**What they are.** Up to $1M USDC, reintroduced Jan 2025. Auri lends USDC against XAUT at 3.75% on Morpho on Ethereum. Adding a gold Morpho market is a configuration change, not a rebuild.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- $50B company, brand, distribution, licences
- Already lending on Morpho at scale

**Where Auri beats them.**
- Only lends against crypto, not gold
- Serves traders, not savers

**Positioning.** The differentiator vs Coinbase can't be rate — it's the collateral (gold) and the customer (savers). Also: Coinbase-on-Morpho validates Auri's rails.

**Watch for.** THE fastest-moving threat in the deck: Coinbase adds XAUT as collateral.
