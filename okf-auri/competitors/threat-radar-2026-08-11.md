**Implication unchanged:** Auri's Canada window is real but time-boxed; the US
may have no window at all. This remains the joint-most-urgent threat.

- So the single fastest threat in the deck has **not** materialised. Auri's
  window is still open — but the move remains a configuration change, not a
  rebuild.

## 3. New signal — a gold Morpho market already exists

- **Morpho already runs a PAXG/USDC market (~$1.71M active loans, PAXG
  collateral)** as of early 2026, and the tokenized-gold sector exceeded **$5B**
  (PAXG + XAUT dominate).
- Two readings: (a) the gold-on-Morpho path is **proven and live** for PAXG,
  validating Auri's exact technical bet; (b) it is also a signal that gold
  collateral markets are actively being stood up — the space Auri wants to own
  is warming up on the same rail.

## Net

Both severe threats remain **latent, not realised** — Auri's window is open as
of 2026-08-11. The technical rail (gold on Morpho) is now demonstrably live for
PAXG, which strengthens the "our rails are proven" story to investors and
