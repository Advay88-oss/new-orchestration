# Indian gold loans (Muthoot, Rupeek, IIFL)

The mature, mass-market gold-loan industry — 8-15% APR. Auri at 3.75% is 2-4x cheaper than the entire Indian industry.

**What they are.** Rupeek, indiagold, IIFL, Muthoot, Manappuram — branch-heavy, digital apps disbursing in <60s against gold in a digital locker. The sharpest message for the Indian diaspora in the US and Canada.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Massive scale, deep trust, physical branches

**Where Auri beats them.**
- 3.75% vs 8-15%; self-custody; cross-border

**Positioning.** 2-4x cheaper than the entire Indian gold-loan industry — the diaspora knows exactly what a gold loan should cost.

**Watch for.** India expansion (digital-gold rules in flux).
