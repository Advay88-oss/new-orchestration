# Nexo

Crypto-backed credit lines on 100+ assets, 1.9-18.9% APR. Relaunched in the US in 2026 (excl. New York).

**What they are.** $50-$2M. Broad asset support, established. Crypto collateral only.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Breadth of assets, established, high limits

**Where Auri beats them.**
- No gold; crypto-native audience

**Positioning.** Everywhere in crypto lending — but never against gold.

**Watch for.** If Nexo adds tokenized gold.
