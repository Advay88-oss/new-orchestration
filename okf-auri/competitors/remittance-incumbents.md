# Wise / Remitly / Western Union

Remittance incumbents. Auri doesn't beat them on price — it makes remittance a side effect of holding gold, and every gift-claim link a viral acquisition loop.

**What they are.** US is the largest source of outbound remittances (~$93B formal 2024, up to $230B with informal). India received ~$137-138B, Mexico ~$68B. Global flows $857B (2025) → $900B+ (2026). ⚠ US 1% remittance excise levy — open question whether gold self-custody transfers are in scope; needs counsel.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Ubiquity, price, cash-out network

**Where Auri beats them.**
- Remittance as a side effect of gold + viral gift loops

**Positioning.** Absorb remittance as a growth loop, don't fight on price.

**Watch for.** IRS/Treasury guidance on the 1% excise applied to gold transfers.
