# Kinesis Money

The most feature-complete gold-money platform and the most direct philosophical rival — and it pays a yield on gold.

**What they are.** Gold/silver tokens (KAU/KAG), debit card, and uniquely pays yield (57.5% of platform fees back to users, six monthly yields in physical gold). 0.22% fee — cheapest in category.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Yield on gold (Auri has no equivalent — a real gap)
- Cheapest fees
- Gold AND silver
- Live card, operating history

**Where Auri beats them.**
- No borrowing against holdings
- Closed proprietary system, not standard DeFi rails
- No developer API
- Crypto-flavoured/complex UX vs Auri's Web2-clean

**Positioning.** Kinesis is what Auri looks like if it wins, minus credit and minus self-custody. Their yield mechanic is the best idea in the set.

**Watch for.** If Kinesis adds borrowing or self-custody.
