# Wealthsimple Gold

The most dangerous competitor in Canada — physically-backed gold trading inside an app millions already trust.

**What they are.** Gold from $1 CAD, 24/7, fractional, real-time, instant. 1% trading fee, $0 storage, custody via Silver Gold Bull, redeemable for Royal Canadian Mint coins, TFSA/RRSP-eligible.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Millions of trusting Canadian users
- Tax-sheltered accounts (TFSA/RRSP)
- Physical coin redemption
- Regulated brokerage, no liquidation risk to explain
- $1 minimum, no extra KYC friction

**Where Auri beats them.**
- Cannot borrow against gold — Auri's entire credit product doesn't exist there
- Cannot spend it (no card)
- Cannot send/gift it
- Custodial — you trust their balance sheet, not your keys
- No developer API

**Positioning.** Wealthsimple lets you own gold. Auri lets you use it. Fight on borrow/spend/send/gift/custody, never on brand or trust.

**Watch for.** Nightmare scenario: Wealthsimple adds a gold-backed line of credit. They have the balance sheet and the licence.
