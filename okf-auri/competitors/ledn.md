# Ledn

The most important competitor to watch — Tether partnered with Ledn to let XAUT holders borrow against gold. Literally Auri's core product, from the token issuer.

**What they are.** Toronto, BTC-only (dropped ETH late 2025), max LTV 50%, not in QC/NB/NS/SK. The Tether×Ledn XAUT loan product will NOT be available in Canada or the EU at launch — but the US is fair game. Rates/LTV undisclosed, expected later 2026.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Token issuer (Tether) behind it
- Established Canadian lender distribution

**Where Auri beats them.**
- BTC-only today (their own product)
- XAUT loan excludes Canada/EU at launch — Auri's temporary window
- Custodial, no self-custody
- No gold buying/spending/gifting

**Positioning.** Auri's window is real but time-boxed. In Canada it's regulatory and temporary; in the US there may be no window — only a race.

**Watch for.** Tether×Ledn XAUT loan launch and its jurisdiction list. THE most urgent threat alongside Coinbase.
