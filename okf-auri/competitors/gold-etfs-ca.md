# Gold ETFs (CGL / CGL.C)

The default for any financially literate Canadian — commission-free, TFSA/RRSP-eligible paper gold.

**What they are.** Commission-free on Wealthsimple and Questrade. No custody, no spending, no borrowing, no gifting — a paper claim in a brokerage account.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Tax-sheltered, familiar, liquid

**Where Auri beats them.**
- You own a fund that owns gold — you can't do anything with it

**Positioning.** You don't own gold, you own a fund that owns gold.
