# Vaulted

Simplest US vaulted-gold app — one all-in 1.8% fee, $10 minimum (the same entry point Auri markets).

**What they are.** 1.8% all-in of wholesale gold price; $10 min — lowest US entry point. Custodial, buy-and-hold only.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- $10 minimum, dead simple

**Where Auri beats them.**
- Buy-and-hold only, no use

**Positioning.** A warehouse with a low door.
