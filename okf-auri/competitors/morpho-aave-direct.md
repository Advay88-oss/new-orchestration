# Morpho / Aave (direct DeFi)

A sophisticated user can do exactly what Auri does, manually, for less — but ~99.9% of the target market never will.

**What they are.** Needs a wallet, gas, LTV understanding, and docs-first UI tolerance. Auri is the abstraction over this.

**Where they beat Auri.**
- Cheaper, permissionless, for experts

**Where Auri beats them.**
- No wallet/gas/complexity for the user

**Positioning.** Auri is the Web2 front door to the same rails.
