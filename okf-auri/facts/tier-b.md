# Tier B - qualified

True, but never state without the qualifier:

- **3.75% APR** credit line - a rate that can change; borrowing carries **liquidation risk** if gold drops.
- **Up to 5x leverage** - amplifies losses as hard as gains; real liquidation risk; "built for pros".
- **Gold price** (e.g. ~CA$5,700/oz, Jul 2026) is time-stamped and moves; never a promise of value.
- Any "borrow / spend / grow your gold" claim must not imply a guaranteed return.
