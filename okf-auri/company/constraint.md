# Overriding constraint

**Auri deals in real money and real risk. Gold can fall in value. Borrowing against gold carries liquidation risk. Leverage multiplies losses.**

Every claim must respect this:

- Never promise or imply a return, growth, or safety. Gold is a store of value, not a yield.
- Any mention of borrowing, a credit line, or leverage must carry its risk in the same breath - the site itself always does ("if gold drops sharply, some of it can be liquidated").
- Custody claims (non-custodial, "Auri holds $0", "in your name") must match the published proof, never exceed it.
- Numbers (tonnes, bars, APR, price) must match the current attestation. Stale or rounded figures are false.

This is Auri's overriding stage constraint. It outranks engagement, cleverness, and any persona.
