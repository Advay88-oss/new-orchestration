# Store of value

**Gold never needed rescuing. It needed rails.**

Your dollars buy a little less every year. Gold has held value for 5,000 years - it just couldn't move. Never phrase this as a promised return; gold can fall.

Against [use your gold](/arcs/use-your-gold.md): usage is the feature, preservation is the reason.
Against [self-custody](/arcs/self-custody.md): custody is how, preservation is why.
