# Gold you can use

**Anyone can hold gold. Auri lets you use it.**

Bars in a vault can't buy coffee, cross a border, or back a loan. The scarce thing isn't owning gold - it's using it. Spend on the card, send by link, borrow against it (with its liquidation risk stated).

Against [self-custody](/arcs/self-custody.md): custody is trust plumbing; the felt magic is tapping gold at a checkout.
Against [store of value](/arcs/store-of-value.md): everyone knows gold holds value - the news is that it finally moves.
