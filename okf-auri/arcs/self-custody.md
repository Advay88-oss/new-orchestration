# Non-custodial

**If Auri vanished tonight, your gold's still yours in the morning.**

Banks lend your money and score you for it. Auri holds $0 - your gold sits in a Swiss vault in your own name, attested quarterly, not on Auri's books.

Against [use your gold](/arcs/use-your-gold.md): utility means nothing if the custodian can lose it.
Against [store of value](/arcs/store-of-value.md): value you don't truly own isn't a store, it's an IOU.
