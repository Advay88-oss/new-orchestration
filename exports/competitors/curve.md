## 1. Executive Summary & 0-to-100 User Playbook

Curve (originally StableSwap) addressed the acute problem of stablecoin slippage in early 2020. Michael Egorov published a paper showing a custom bonding curve that was 100x-300x more capital efficient than Uniswap for assets pegged to the same value (DAI/USDC/USDT). Early tweets focused purely on showing slippage comparisons: swapping $100K on Uniswap lost $2,000 to slippage; on Curve it lost $10. DeFi traders immediately migrated.
