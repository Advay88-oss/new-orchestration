## 1. Executive Summary & 0-to-100 User Playbook

Morpho's early Twitter GTM was one of the most intellectually rigorous in DeFi history. Instead of asking users to trust a new lending pool, Paul Frambot and team announced Morpho-Compound: a protocol that improved rates on Compound without adding liquidity risk. If matched peer-to-peer, both parties got better rates; if unmatched, funds fell back to Compound. This zero-downside thesis convinced early Aave and Compound whales to deposit hundreds of millions.
