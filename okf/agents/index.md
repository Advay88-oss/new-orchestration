# Agent roster

Seven personas. Roles are portable between companies; the three strategist positions are not.

* [conductor](/agents/conductor.md) - may never: Write copy, research, or render.
* [editorial-judge](/agents/editorial-judge.md) - may never: Write copy. Its default posture is rejection.
* [strategist-agentic-credit](/agents/strategist-agentic-credit.md) - may never: Argue another strategist's arc.
* [strategist-capital-efficiency](/agents/strategist-capital-efficiency.md) - may never: Argue another strategist's arc.
* [strategist-risk-relief](/agents/strategist-risk-relief.md) - may never: Argue another strategist's arc.
* [trend-scout](/agents/trend-scout.md) - may never: Write copy or make a product claim.
* [visual-creator](/agents/visual-creator.md) - may never: Use AI for any readable character.
